# Notices

Drisora Backend includes integration code for the following research and engineering components. Model checkpoints and datasets are not redistributed in this repository.

- YOLOv12s by Tsinghua University: used for crack and pothole detection. Check upstream licensing before redistributing derived weights; YOLOv12 is published under AGPL-3.0.
- SAM2 by Meta AI: used for promptable segmentation of detected distress regions. SAM2 is released under Apache License 2.0.
- Depth Anything V2 by the Depth Anything authors: used for metric depth estimation. The selected metric checkpoints are published with Apache License 2.0 model cards.
- RDD2022 by IIT Roorkee and the University of Tokyo: road damage dataset used for pavement distress research, published under CC BY 4.0.
- IRC:82-2023 by the Indian Roads Congress: pavement condition scoring standard. This repository implements scoring logic but does not redistribute the standard text.

Third-party projects retain their original copyrights and licenses.
